Bridging Engineering
and Intelligence

I am a Lecturer in Computer Science at the Faculty of Engineering of the University of Porto (FEUP) and a Researcher at INESC TEC, one of Portugal's leading research institutes in information technologies and electronics.

My academic journey spans Electronic Engineering — where I developed a strong foundation in signal processing and embedded systems — to Computer Science and Informatics Engineering, where I now design and study intelligent algorithms for language understanding, visual perception, and sequential decision making.

I am currently pursuing a second doctorate in Informatics Engineering, driven by open questions at the frontier of machine learning and autonomous systems. I believe that durable intelligence emerges from the dialogue between rigorous theory and hands-on engineering.
